3. Android Device Permissions & Zero Broad Storage

hlwgys adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege. The application requests only permissions strictly necessary for its features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only when you choose to capture a new photo or record a video reel directly within the app. Camera access is optional (required="false") and never runs in the background.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used only while recording video reels or voice audio with explicit user initiation.
  • Zero Broad Storage Permissions: In full compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies, hlwgys never requests broad device storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). All photo and video selections use the modern, zero-permission Android Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia).
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to transmit and receive feed posts, video reels, user stories, messages, and synchronize data with our secure cloud database.
  • Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used to provide pleasant haptic feedback when double-tapping to like posts, sending chat messages, or toggling settings.

5. Third-Party Services & Cloud Infrastructure

hlwgys utilizes trusted, industry-standard third-party service providers:

  • Supabase: Provides backend infrastructure including PostgreSQL database and user authentication. Data stored in Supabase is secured using Row-Level Security (RLS) policies to ensure that only authorized users can access or modify their content.
  • Apple Music / iTunes Public Preview CDNs: Used solely to stream short 30-second music sample previews for curated background tracks in video reels.
  • No Analytics Trackers or Ad Networks: hlwgys does not embed third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking trackers, or monetization telemetry.

6. Data Retention, Auto-Expiration & Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal data:

  • Post & Reel Deletion: You have full control to edit or permanently delete your posts, reels, stories, comments, and uploaded audio tracks at any time within the app interface.
  • In-App Cache Clearing: You can purge all locally cached media, temporary files, and session credentials with a single tap in Profile → Clear Cache Files & Stored Data.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data. You can delete your account by contacting us at pallabjyotisaikia321@gmail.com with your registered email or username. All associated profiles, posts, reels, comments, and messages will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days of verified request.
